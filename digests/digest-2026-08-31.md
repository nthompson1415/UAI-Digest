**2. PILOT in the Loop: Live Self-Improvement for Long-Horizon Agents**
A new system called PILOT enables AI agents to improve themselves in real-time during long-running tasks, rather than just after a task is completed. This 'live' self-improvement allows agents to redirect their current actions and learn more efficiently, leading to significant improvements in task success rates and reduced computational costs.
⚠️ [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26530) · 2026-08-27

**3. Towards a Mathematical Theory of Superposition in Neural Networks**
This paper develops a mathematical framework to understand superposition in neural networks, using tools from frame theory and compressed sensing. It proposes a model for how sparse data is encoded and recovered, offering theoretical guarantees for feature recovery and analyzing its performance with different types of matrices.

**4. Riemann-1.0: An Embodied World Action Model for Physical AI**

It achieved a high success rate on real-world robot manipulation tasks and improved performance on a simulation benchmark, demonstrating its potential for physical AI applications.
